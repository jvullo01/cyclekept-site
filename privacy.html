Privacy Policy

Cyclekept · Your cycle belongs to you.

The short version

Data Not Collected

Cyclekept doesn't collect any information about you. There's no account, no ads, no analytics, no crash reporting and no tracking. There are no third-party tools or SDKs in the app, and there are no servers for it to talk to. We never see your cycle data.

Your data stays only on this iPhone

Everything you log stays in Cyclekept on your iPhone: period and spotting days, symptoms, moods, notes, reminders and settings.

  • Never in iCloud or backups. Cyclekept doesn't sync to iCloud, and its data is excluded from iCloud and computer backups of your iPhone.
  • Protected on the device. The data is stored with iOS file protection, so it can't be read while your iPhone is locked. You can also turn on an app lock (Face ID, Touch ID or your passcode).
  • Widgets show only "Open Cyclekept" while your iPhone is locked. Cycle details on widgets are off unless you turn them on.
  • Reminders are notifications scheduled on your iPhone. Their wording is discreet by default.

The encrypted export is the only backup

Because Cyclekept is never in iCloud or iPhone backups, the only way to back up or move your data is Export encrypted backup in Privacy > Your data. It creates a password-protected file that you save wherever you choose. We never receive it, and we can't recover a forgotten password. If you lose your iPhone without an export, your Cyclekept data can't be restored.

You can also export a CSV spreadsheet or a PDF report for your doctor. Those files are not encrypted; where they go is up to you.

Apple Health is optional and off by default

Connecting Apple Health is up to you. If you turn on Save to Apple Health, Cyclekept writes only the period flow and spotting days you log. Symptoms, moods and notes are never written. If you tap Import from Apple Health, Cyclekept reads only period flow and spotting.

Health data is never sent to us or anyone else, and never used for advertising, marketing or data mining. Days you choose to save to Apple Health are then kept by the Health app under your Health settings. You can change or remove access any time in the Health app or iOS Settings.

On-device AI

With Cyclekept Pro, the monthly summary is written by Apple's on-device model on iPhones with Apple Intelligence turned on. It runs entirely on your iPhone and never uses a server, including Apple's Private Cloud Compute. If it isn't available, Cyclekept shows a plain summary instead. Summaries are not medical advice.

Purchases

Cyclekept Pro is an optional one-time in-app purchase. Apple handles the payment; we never see your name, email, card or Apple Account details.

Deleting your data

Privacy > Erase all Cyclekept data deletes everything Cyclekept stored on your iPhone. This can't be undone. Deleting the app does the same. Days you saved to Apple Health stay in Health unless you delete them there (or choose the option to remove them when you erase).

Children

Cyclekept collects no personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes and contact

If this policy ever changes, the new version will be posted here with a new effective date. If Cyclekept ever starts collecting anything, this policy and the App Store privacy label will say so first.
